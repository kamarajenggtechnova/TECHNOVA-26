1
Autonomous Smart Drain Cleaning and Blockage Detection System
◈ PROBLEM STATEMENT
Urban and semi-urban areas face frequent flooding due to clogged drainage systems caused by plastic waste, sludge accumulation, and lack of regular inspection. Manual cleaning exposes workers to hazardous gases and unsafe conditions.
◈ CHALLENGE
Design an autonomous or semi-autonomous robotic system capable of:
  • Detecting drain blockages
  • Removing solid waste
  • Monitoring gas levels and water flow conditions
◈ EXPECTED CONCEPTS
  • Mechanical waste collection mechanism
  • Motorized mobility in narrow drains
  • IoT-based monitoring
  • Sensors (gas, ultrasonic, vision)
  • Remote operation or autonomous navigation
2
Smart Public Toilet Hygiene Monitoring and Self-Cleaning System
◈ PROBLEM STATEMENT
Public toilets often become unusable due to poor maintenance, water wastage, and lack of real-time monitoring, creating major sanitation challenges.
◈ CHALLENGE
Develop a self-maintaining smart sanitation unit that:
  • Monitors cleanliness automatically
  • Performs automated floor/seat cleaning
  • Optimizes water usage
  • Alerts authorities when maintenance is required
◈ EXPECTED CONCEPTS
  • Mechanical cleaning mechanisms
  • Automated spray and drying systems
  • Sensor-based contamination detection
  • Mechatronic control using microcontrollers
  • IoT dashboard
3
Smart Low-Cost Patient Transfer and Lifting Assistance System
◈ PROBLEM STATEMENT
Hospitals and rural healthcare centers face difficulties in safely transferring patients between beds, wheelchairs, and stretchers. Manual lifting causes injuries to caregivers and discomfort to patients.
◈ CHALLENGE
Design an assistive patient transfer system that:
  • Reduces manual lifting effort
  • Automatically adjusts height and orientation
  • Ensures safe patient handling
◈ EXPECTED CONCEPTS
  • Mechanical lifting mechanisms (scissor lift/hydraulic/electric actuator)
  • Load sensing and safety locking
  • Motorized control with embedded system
  • User-friendly interface
4
Interactive Mechatronics-Based Learning Desk for STEM Education
◈ PROBLEM STATEMENT
Many schools lack hands-on learning tools, limiting practical understanding of engineering concepts.
◈ CHALLENGE
Develop a modular smart learning desk that:
  • Demonstrates mechanical mechanisms interactively
  • Includes programmable sensors and actuators
  • Supports multiple STEM experiments
◈ EXPECTED CONCEPTS
  • Mechanical linkage demonstrations
  • Plug-and-play electronics modules
  • Embedded programming interface
  • AR/visual feedback integration
5
Automated Examination Paper Handling and Sorting System
◈ PROBLEM STATEMENT
Manual handling of examination papers causes delays, errors, and security concerns.
◈ CHALLENGE
Develop a system that:
  • Automatically sorts answer sheets
  • Counts and organizes papers securely
  • Tracks movement digitally
◈ EXPECTED CONCEPTS
  • Conveyor and mechanical sorting system
  • Optical recognition
  • Embedded control
  • Secure data logging
1
Cost-Effective Prefabricated Housing for Tribal Communities
◈ CHALLENGE
How might we design cost-effective, prefabricated housing structures for tribal communities in interior forest areas, ensuring durability, environmental sustainability, and easy assembly in remote locations?
2
Automated Water Distribution System for Rural Areas
◈ CHALLENGE
How might we develop an automated water distribution system that ensures 55 LPCD per person, prevents wastage, and reduces electricity usage in rural households?
3
Home-Level Solid Waste and E-Waste Disposal Technology
◈ CHALLENGE
Design a home-level cost-effective safety technology to dispose of solid waste and e-waste without affecting the environment.
4
Extendable Safety Tool for Sanitation Workers
◈ CHALLENGE
How might we design an extendable, adjustable-hand fork for sanitation workers (Thooimai Paniyalargal) in rural areas, allowing them to safely and efficiently separate plastic waste from organic waste while minimizing direct contact and improving safety and comfort?
5
Farm Produce Storage Solutions for Small Farmers
◈ CHALLENGE
How might we develop sound, cost-effective storage and warehousing solutions for storing farm produce which can be easily accessed by poorer farmers and can be scaled up to mass production?
